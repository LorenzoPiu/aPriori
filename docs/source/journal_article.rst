Article preprint
========================

A journal article describing the methodology, implementation, and
applications of the framework is available as a preprint on arXiv.
The paper presents the motivation behind the project, details the data
processing pipeline, and demonstrates how the generated datasets can be
used for machine-learning–based modeling of three-dimensional physical
fields. The article also discusses reproducibility considerations and
provides example workflows consistent with the documentation.

You can **access** and **download** the full preprint here:

`Preprint page on arXiv <https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24363>`_
